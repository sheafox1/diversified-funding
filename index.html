How It Works

A simple process from first call to funding

We use our real estate experience to keep the process straightforward. Rates start as low as prime + 1.

1. Talk with us

Present your deal in a short phone call or meeting with one of our real estate professionals.

2. Feasibility review

We review the property, your project analysis and your financial statements to see if it fits.

3. Financing proposal

We prepare a proposal with specific terms, rates and conditions tailored to your project.

4. Close and fund

We use our own funds and make our own decisions, so closing and funding move without red tape.

Underwriting

Loans are collateral based, not credit driven

We look for borrowers with demonstrated experience who can confidently follow through on their business plan, with “skin in the game” and sufficient collateral. If you are new, an experienced team around you can make a difference.
